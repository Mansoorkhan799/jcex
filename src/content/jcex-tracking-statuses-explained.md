# JCEX Tracking Statuses Explained: Customs, Arrived, Out for Delivery & More

JCEX tracking messages can look confusing — especially when the same parcel shows **customs**, **arrived at destination**, then goes quiet for days. Status text comes from JCEX, airlines, brokers, and last-mile partners, so wording varies by route. Understanding each stage helps you know whether to wait, pay a duty, or contact support.

This glossary explains the most common **JCEX tracking statuses**, what they mean in real life, and what you should do next. Track your number anytime on [**JCEXTracking.net**](/).

| In transit / Departed from airport | Line-haul | Moving between hubs or countries |

## Pre-shipment & Origin Statuses

### Info received / Shipment information received / Label created

The seller or warehouse created a tracking number. **No physical scan** may exist yet.

**What to do:** Wait **3–7 days** for the first acceptance scan. If nothing appears, ask the seller for dispatch proof. Related: [no updates yet](/jcex-tracking-shows-no-updates-yet).

### Picked up / Accepted / Arrived at sorting center

JCEX or a partner has the parcel. Sorting and consolidation toward an export gateway begin.

**What to do:** No action needed unless the status freezes past your service’s normal pattern.

## International Transit Statuses

### In transit / Departed from origin / Handed over to airline

The package left the origin network or boarded international transport. **Multi-day silence is normal** while the parcel is on a plane or in a consolidated container.

**What to do:** Keep tracking weekly. Long gaps alone do not mean the parcel is lost — see [no updates yet](/jcex-tracking-shows-no-updates-yet).

### Arrived at transit hub / Departed facility

Intermediate scans at airports or regional hubs. Useful for confirming movement, but not a delivery ETA.

## Customs Statuses Explained

### Export customs clearance / Customs declaration (origin)

Origin authorities or brokers check documents and security before international departure.

**What to do:** Usually wait. Sellers may need to fix invoices if clearance stalls.

### Arrived at destination country / Inbound mail / Arrived at gateway

The shipment reached your country’s gateway or exchange office. **This does not automatically mean customs is finished** or that a local driver has the parcel.

**What to do:** Watch for import-customs or local-carrier scans. Check email/SMS for duty notices.

### Import customs / Item presented to customs / Customs clearance

Destination customs is reviewing the goods — docs, inspection, duties, or restricted-item checks.

**What to do:**

- Respond only through **official** carrier or customs channels
- Ask the seller for a commercial invoice if requested
- Pay duties only on verified portals — never via random text links

### Released from customs / Customs cleared

Import checks finished. The parcel can move to a destination carrier or delivery depot.

**What to do:** Expect a handoff or local tracking number soon.

## Last-Mile Statuses

### Handed over to local carrier / Accepted by destination partner

JCEX’s international leg is done. USPS, Royal Mail, Deutsche Post, PostNL, or another partner now owns delivery. A **new last-mile tracking number** may appear.

**What to do:** Track that local number on the partner’s site and on [JCEXTracking.net](/).

### At local delivery office / Arrived at destination facility

The parcel is in your city or regional depot, preparing for an attempt.

### Out for delivery

The package is on a delivery vehicle for an attempt today (or the carrier’s next run).

**What to do:** Ensure someone can receive it, unlock gates, and watch for locker/pickup codes. Failed attempts often move to a hold location quickly.

### Delivered / Signed for / Delivered to mailbox / Locker

The logistics chain closed the shipment. Delivery may be to your door, mailbox, locker, reception, or a neighbor.

**What to do if you have nothing:** Follow [delivered but not received](/jcex-delivered-but-not-received).

## Exception & Problem Statuses

### Delivery failed / Attempted delivery / Exception

Address issues, recipient unavailable, access problems, unpaid charges, or weather.

**What to do:** Contact the **last-mile carrier** immediately for redelivery or pickup before the hold period ends.

### Held / Pending / Awaiting collection

Parcel is waiting for pickup, payment, or address confirmation.

**What to do:** Read the full event text for location and deadline.

### Returned to sender

Delivery or customs release failed and the parcel is going back.

**What to do:** Contact the seller for a reshipment or refund path; returns can take weeks.

## Typical Status Progression (Healthy Shipment)

1. Info received  
2. Accepted / sorted at origin  
3. Export customs → departed  
4. International transit (possible quiet gap)  
5. Arrived at destination country  
6. Import customs → released  
7. Handed to local carrier  
8. Out for delivery  
9. Delivered  

Not every shipment shows every step. Economy lanes may skip detailed mid-journey scans.

## FAQs

### Does “arrived at destination country” mean I get it tomorrow?

No. Customs and last-mile handoff can still take several days.

### Why does my status switch languages or carrier names?

Partner systems inject their own event text. A carrier name change usually means a handoff, not a new order.

### Which status means I should open a dispute?

Dispute when the seller’s delivery promise is exceeded, tracking shows delivery elsewhere, or the parcel is returned/lost — not merely because of a normal customs or transit pause.

## Final Thoughts

**JCEX tracking statuses** are a map of who currently holds your parcel — warehouse, customs, airline, or local courier. “Arrived,” “customs,” and “out for delivery” each mean something different; reading them correctly prevents unnecessary panic and helps you act only when the status truly needs action.
