# Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy of [jcextracking.net](/)

At [JCEX Tracking](/), your privacy matters. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you use our tracking services.

**Information we collect:**

- Tracking numbers you enter to check shipments
- Optional contact details you provide via forms or email
- Cookies and analytics data used to improve the website experience

**How we use your information:**

- To provide tracking updates and related notifications
- To respond to questions or feedback
- To improve website functionality and reliability

**Data protection:**

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data when required by law or with service providers needed to operate the tracking service.

**Cookies:**

We use cookies to enhance experience, monitor performance, and understand how the site is used.

**Your rights:**

You can request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at [contact@jcextracking.net](mailto:contact@jcextracking.net).

**Updates:**

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
